Introduction

Jaipur is a beautiful city known for its forts, palaces and colorful markets.

History

Jaipur was founded in 1727 and is known as the Pink City.

Famous Places

Some famous places include Amber Fort, City Palace and Hawa Mahal.

Food

Jaipur is also known for traditional Rajasthani food and sweets.
